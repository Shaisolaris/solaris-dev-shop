# FAQ

## Is this all of Solaris?

No. This is Solaris Dev Shop 0.52. It is the public edition of the V5.2.2 employees: 61 directories plus the original chief of staff.

## Why not 73, 58, or 52?

Those numbers come from older catalogs and prose on the private pin. The count used here is one `SKILL.md` at `employees/<department>/<name>/SKILL.md`. That count is 61. Nested diagnostic skills under WordPress Master are not extra employees.

## Is the chief of staff a 62nd employee in the table?

No. The chief of staff lives at `chief-of-staff/`. It assigns work. It is not listed as a specialist.

## Can I publish this fork with my own secrets?

No. Do not commit passwords, API keys, tokens, or real accounts. Put a placeholder and keep the value outside the repository.

## Where is the marketplace?

Not in this repository. A later commercial product is out of scope here.
