# LinkedIn + social depth (2026) - reference

METHODOLOGY absorption (2026-06-13, web research; sources: Richard van der Blom Algorithm Insights, Teract/Sprout/DataSlayer algo analyses, Justin Welsh, Jasmin Alic, LinkedIn B2Believe). Tech-first, general-applicable.

## 1. The 2026 LinkedIn algorithm (mental model)
- Shifted from a relationship graph to an INTEREST graph: distribution is driven by your TOPIC DNA (consistency of subject), not network size.
- **3-stage distribution:** (1) Initial 0-60 min → ~2-5% of network; if 5-10% engage you advance, <2% kills it. The first 60 minutes decide everything. (2) Expanded 1-6 hrs → 10-20% + 2nd-degree; NLP now judges comment quality + dwell time. (3) Viral 6+ hrs → top <1% reach beyond network; needs sustained 24h+ engagement.
- **7 ranking signals (weighted):** dwell time (HIGHEST - scroll depth/viewport/'see more') > comment QUALITY (NLP; <10-word comments ~worthless) > shares-with-commentary > engagement RATE not count > author authority > semantic relevance > likes (lowest). SAVES are now ~5x a like - the strongest "valuable" signal.
- **Negative signals (cut reach 50-70%):** external links in the body (put link in first comment) · engagement bait ("tag someone") · rapid posting (space 4-6h, ~1/day) · formulaic AI content (~47% less reach; AI-as-assistant is fine, AI-as-replacement throttled) · engagement pods (detection ~97%, now PERMANENT reach reduction - dead tactic).
- Rule: LinkedIn optimizes for time-on-platform + substantive conversation. Write to be READ longer and REPLIED to with something real.

## 2. Formats that win (with benchmarks)
- Text-only = highest reach (6-8% for quality), 150-300 words (forces the 'see more' → dwell). Document/PDF carousels = highest engagement (~6.6%) + 2-3x dwell, 6-10 slides one-idea-each. Native video = lower initial reach but 2-3x dwell; 2026 rewards long-form retention. Single image = 20-30% LESS than text unless it adds value (chart/screenshot, never stock). Link posts lowest (1-2%) - link in first comment.

## 3. Hooks (the first 1-2 lines are the game)
- Two-line hook: line 1 = hook <=8 words; line 2 = curiosity-gap preview <=12 words teasing a concrete payoff.
- Types: contrarian statement · specific number + payoff · the "exact" framing · negative-event→positive-outcome arc · hero's journey.
- Rules: <=8 words line 1; never open "stop/don't/quit"; never bait-and-switch (NLP detects + penalizes hook/body mismatch); hint don't spoil.
- **Structure:** Hook (1-2) → Context (3-8, specific data) → Insight (9-15, your unique data/lesson) → CTA (a SPECIFIC question, never "what do you think?"). Line break every 1-2 sentences, 1-2 emojis max, 0-2 hashtags (semantic understanding made stuffing redundant).

## 4. Cadence + timing
- 3-5x/week sweet spot; frequency > timing for momentum BUT daily at 2% engagement is worse than 3x/week at 8% (density > volume).
- US/B2B best: Tue-Thu 7-9am or 12-1pm ET. Avoid weekends/evenings. Caveat: check YOUR audience-online analytics - generic times are a myth.

## 5. Personal brand + commenting (the highest-ROI levers)
- Personal profiles out-reach company pages 5-10x; inbound-from-content converts ~14.6% vs ~1.7% cold; 82% of B2B buyers say individual thought-leader content influences purchasing. Build a ROSTER (CEO + sales leaders + SMEs), not one hero. Study: Justin Welsh (engages every comment in the first hour; content→profile→newsletter→product funnel), Jasmin Alic (hook/credential/short-copy rules), van der Blom (the annual data report).
- **Strategic commenting beats posting for NEW reach:** comment 10-15x/day on accounts 2-10x your size, within the first 30 min, adding real value → ~50-100 profile visits/day, 10-20 new followers/week. On your own posts: reply to every comment within 30 min (keeps velocity high in the make-or-break first hour).

## 6. Content → pipeline (signal-based)
- Publish what your ICP engages → track who comments/reacts on relevant-topic posts (intent signal) → DM the engagers with a personalized note referencing what they engaged with (NOT a pitch). Content-driven DMs ~10% response (~2x cold). Profile-as-funnel (Welsh): headline = the outcome you deliver, featured = lead capture, about = the transformation. Measure cost-per-target-account-engaged + cost-per-SQL, not followers.
- Company pages (secondary): SEO/credibility surface, employee-advocacy hub, LinkedIn newsletters (25-35% open vs ~21% email), thought-leader ads (sponsor an individual's post).

## 7. Beyond LinkedIn
- **X/Twitter (founders):** 70/30 rule - 70% strategic replies to high-follower accounts (replies ~15x the weight of likes), 30% original; threads 4-8 tweets; X Premium for For-You priority; the "reply guy" path is the fastest cold start; consistency is the moat (most quit month 2).
- **YouTube (tech-news channel):** title keyword in first 60 chars + emotional hook (optimize to win the click in comparison, not to describe); thumbnail 3-4 words big text + emotive face + high contrast (5-10% CTR); retention 65-75% is the promotion benchmark, hook in first 5s; Shorts = independent algorithm, +40% channel discovery, top-of-funnel for long-form.
- **Short-form (Reels/Shorts/TikTok):** hook 0-3s → value 3-50s → CTA 50-60s; B2B hooks = authority/specific-percentage/pain/curiosity/objection; 12+ Shorts/month → ~35% lower CPL; 96% of B2B marketers report positive short-form ROI.
