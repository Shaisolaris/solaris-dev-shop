# Content strategy + taste + distribution (2026) - reference

METHODOLOGY absorption (2026-06-13, web research; sources: CMI 2026 trends, B2BNN, RevvGrowth, TopRank, WARC, ContentBeta, Averi, Onely). Tech-first, general.

## 1. Content pillars (the organizing system)
Pick 3-5 pillars at the intersection of your value prop + your audience's top challenges - this trains your "topic DNA" so algorithms and LLMs categorize you cleanly. Reliable tech set: (1) industry insight/POV (contrarian-but-defensible takes) · (2) how-to/frameworks (the saveable workhorse) · (3) behind-the-build / building-in-public · (4) proof (case studies, results, ORIGINAL data) · (5) founder/personal narrative. 47% of B2B marketers are increasing original research in 2026 - proprietary data is the single most defensible content type (nobody can copy it).

## 2. "Show don't tell" for tech (the premium move)
Tech content dies when it describes, wins when it demonstrates: "our tool is fast" → a 12-sec screen recording + the number (3.2s→0.4s); "we value transparency" → publish the actual outage postmortem; "thought leadership" → publish the dataset and let people argue. SPECIFICITY is the tell of quality: "$50K spent, here's CAC by ad format" out-dwells "5 lessons from building a SaaS" - the generic listicle gets scanned, the specific one gets read.

## 3. Premium vs cheap (the taste rubric)
2026 is a "return to quality" - premium environments are 4.3x more effective at consideration, +37% purchase intent; when AI floods the zone, genuine expertise becomes the scarce premium good. Premium = authenticity + lived-in detail (real numbers/names/failures) · editorial rigor (thoughtful layout, one idea per asset, ruthless cuts) · strategic substance (keyword research, calendar mapped to the journey, voice guidelines, quarterly audits) · a POV (premium takes a side; cheap hedges).
- Do's: specific data, contrarian-defensible takes, your own stories, tight formatting, one idea per post, consistent niche.
- Don'ts: stock photos, hashtag stuffing, generic listicles, bait CTAs, topic-hopping, body links, obvious AI tone.

## 4. Repurposing - 1 idea → N (the multiplier)
You don't need 10 ideas, you need 1 great idea in 10 formats across weeks (repurposers see ~40% more output without proportional creation time). Atomic flow: 1 pillar asset (deep blog/talk/podcast/report) → LinkedIn text post + carousel + native video → X thread + short-form clips → newsletter section + email nurture touch → quote graphics/data viz. Each format ADAPTED to its platform's native vibe, never copy-pasted. Repetition isn't redundancy - that's how ideas land.

## 5. Editorial calendar (map to funnel)
TOFU (awareness): social, blog, infographics, podcasts. MOFU (consideration): ebooks, webinars, feature pages, comparisons, live video. BOFU (decision): case studies, pitch decks, pricing pages. Plan around pillars, batch creation, leave ~20% reactive slots for trends/news.

## 6. Content marketing for tech
- **Distribution > creation:** even great insight fails without distribution; multichannel lifts brand favorability ~24% vs single-channel. Shift the time ratio toward ~40% create / 60% distribute. Architecture-driven: every asset feeds the next (long-form proves depth → LinkedIn distributes the POV → podcast/community build familiarity).
- **Thought leadership + exec amplification:** 83% of B2B marketers use LinkedIn as primary distribution; exec-perspective content out-reaches brand content. Turn internal specialists (engineers, PMs, founders) into visible creators. Original research is the differentiator.
- **SEO + AI search (GEO/AEO):** SaaS buyers now discover via ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini - optimize for both. Topical authority via comprehensive clusters (pillar + supporting articles cross-linked). Structure for AI parsing: summary-first, clear entity definitions, FAQ sections, stats WITH sources, comparison tables (LLMs lift these into answers). Programmatic SEO for SaaS = scaled templated pages (comparisons/integrations/use-cases) with genuinely unique data per page.
- **Measure what matters:** dwell/saves/comment-quality/content-attributed pipeline (social); topical coverage + AI-citation presence + pipeline influence (content/SEO) - not volume.
