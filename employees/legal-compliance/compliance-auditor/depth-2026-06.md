# Compliance Auditor - Depth pass 2026-06-13 (methodology only, no code bundled)

## 1. NET-NEW framework: AI governance (EU AI Act + ISO/IEC 42001)
The employee had full coverage of SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI - but ZERO AI-governance coverage. For a company that builds/operates AI employees and ships AI to clients, this is the most material 2026 gap. Added as a first-class framework.

### EU AI Act (regulation - in force, enforcement from Aug 2 2026)
- Risk-tiered: prohibited / high-risk / limited-risk (transparency) / minimal. Scope a client's AI systems into a tier FIRST.
- GPAI (general-purpose AI) model-provider obligations have applied since Aug 2 2025: technical documentation, transparency, copyright policy, training-data summary; systemic-risk models add evaluations, risk mitigation, incident reporting, cybersecurity.
- Enforcement powers (request docs, evaluate, require measures, fine) begin Aug 2 2026. Pre-Aug-2025 GPAI models must be fully compliant by Aug 2 2027.
- Penalties: up to EUR 35M or 7% global annual turnover (prohibited-use tier); lower bands for other breaches.
- High-risk obligations (Arts 9-15): risk management system, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping/logging, transparency to users, human oversight, accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity.
- Compliance-auditor role: scope client AI systems by risk tier; for high-risk, build the Art. 9-15 gap matrix (same gap-matrix shape used for TSC/Annex A); for GPAI providers, check the model-provider obligation set + the Aug 2026 enforcement clock. Route legal interpretation of the Act to legal-advisor; the auditor owns the controls + evidence.

### ISO/IEC 42001:2023 - AI Management System (AIMS), the certifiable companion
- The ISO management-system standard for AI (analogous to ISO 27001 for infosec): establish/implement/maintain/improve an AIMS, with Annex A controls for AI policy, AI risk assessment + impact assessment, data for AI systems, lifecycle, third-party AI.
- It is CERTIFIABLE and increasingly bundled with SOC 2 / ISO 27001 in vendor questionnaires (Fortune 500 buyers now add "ISO 42001 certified or roadmap" clauses). Maps to EU AI Act Arts 9-15 - use it as the management framework that demonstrates systematic AI Act compliance.
- Add ISO 42001 to the common-controls cross-map (Annex A 27001 <-> TSC <-> HIPAA <-> ISO 42001) so one AI-governance control implementation serves both the AIMS cert and AI Act Art. 9-15 evidence.

## 2. When the AI-governance lane applies to Solaris work
- Any client deploying AI in the EU market (or to EU users): scope by AI Act risk tier; if high-risk, the Art. 9-15 controls; if a GPAI provider, the model-provider obligations + Aug 2026 clock.
- Any client wanting to signal trustworthy AI to enterprise buyers: ISO 42001 readiness (the AI analogue of a SOC 2 readiness engagement - same runway/gap-matrix/evidence machinery this employee already runs).
- Solaris itself (builds + operates AI employees): a candidate for an internal AIMS + AI Act self-assessment.
