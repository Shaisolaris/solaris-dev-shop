# In-House Legal Intake & Triage Ops

**Source:** anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins (official Anthropic, Apache-2.0) - `legal` plugin, skills: `triage-nda`, `vendor-check`, `legal-risk-assessment`, `legal-response`, `signature-request`. Methodology lift permitted with attribution.

> Assists with legal workflows; not legal advice. Outputs reviewed by qualified counsel.

## Gate-0 net-new justification
Existing legal-advisor files own clause SUBSTANCE (MSA/SOW/NDA/DPA drafting, LoL carve-outs, IP indemnity, AI-clause depth, redlining). They lack the OPERATIONAL intake/triage layer an in-house team runs daily. Net-new: turns deep drafting knowledge into a triage-and-route operating rhythm.

## Methodology absorbed
1. **NDA triage GREEN/YELLOW/RED:** GREEN signable under delegated authority; YELLOW counsel review (embedded non-solicit/non-compete, unusual term, missing carve-out); RED full review (one-way, IP-grab, indemnity, broad CI). Screen for embedded non-solicits + missing carve-outs first.
2. **Vendor-agreement status check:** consolidated CLM/CRM/email/doc view - signed vs missing (MSA/DPA/SOW), expirations, surviving obligations, gap analysis.
3. **Risk assessment (severity x likelihood):** grid + explicit escalation criteria (senior/outside counsel); document rating + rationale.
4. **Templated legal-response with escalation gates:** DSARs, litigation-hold, vendor legal Qs, NDA requests, subpoenas - from templates, with checks that BLOCK a templated reply when the situation must escalate.
5. **Signature routing pre-checks:** entity names / exhibits / signature blocks; signing order; route for execution.

## Standing orders
Runs on top of the existing cold-start practice-profile interview; triage inherits that engagement's jurisdictions, escalation thresholds, template defaults.
