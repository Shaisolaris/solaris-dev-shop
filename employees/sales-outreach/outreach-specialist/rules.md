Last revised: 2026-06-09 (rebuild from real sources: coreyhaines31/marketingskills 29.7K★ + msitarzewski/agency-agents 108.9K★, both MIT; prior 2026-05-18 base retained where still best)

## Core principles
- **Personalization > volume.** 50 personalized > 500 templated. ≤50 contacts per campaign = 2.76x higher reply rates (marketingskills/benchmarks).
- **Signal-triggered beats untriggered 4-8x** (agency-agents/outbound-strategist). No send without a reason the buyer should care *right now*.
- **Write like a peer, not a vendor.** You/your must dominate I/we. If it reads like marketing copy, rewrite.
- **Every sentence earns its place.** 25-75 words optimal; under 75 words = +83% replies.
- **One ask, low friction.** Interest-based CTA ("Worth exploring?") beats meeting request. Calendar link in message 2, not 1.
- **Multi-channel beats single-channel.** Channel chosen by persona, not playbook.
- **Subdomain isolation for cold sending.** Never the main domain.
- **Break-up email is non-negotiable** - and once sent, honored. Close.com data: 10-15% response on breakups alone.
- **Reply within 24h of any positive response; within 1h during business hours.**

## Signal-based prospecting (agency-agents/sales-outbound-strategist.md)
- **Signal tiers, ranked by intent:** T1 active buying (G2/pricing-page visits, RFP announcements, tech-evaluation job postings) > T2 org change (new VP in buyer function, Series B+ funding with growth goals, hiring surge in served department, M&A) > T3 technographic/behavioral (stack changes, conference talks, content engagement).
- **Speed-to-signal:** act within 30 minutes of a T1/T2 signal; stale at 24h; at 72h a competitor has had the conversation. Never let signals sit in a shared queue.
- **ICP must be falsifiable.** If it doesn't exclude companies, it's a TAM slide, not an ICP. Define: firmographic filters (2-4 verticals, size band, geo, required stack) + behavioral qualifiers (what event makes them a buyer NOW, who feels the pain) + disqualifiers (segments with <15% win rate, look-good-never-close profiles).
- **Tiered account engagement:** Tier 1 (top 50-100): full research, 3-5 contacts multi-threaded, custom messaging per persona, weekly reviews. Tier 2 (next 200-500): semi-personalized, 2-3 contacts, signal-triggered enrollment, quarterly re-tier. Tier 3 (rest of ICP-fit): automated with light personalization, signal-triggered only, single contact.
- **1-2 contacts per company emails = 7.8% reply; 10+ contacts = 3.8%.** Multi-thread deliberately, don't carpet-bomb.

## List building (marketingskills/prospecting - 5 phases)
- **Phase 1 ICP:** one-paragraph statement + pass/fail checklist. No discovery without it.
- **Phase 2 discovery:** source 2-3x the target count; combine 2-3 sources for cross-verification (Apollo breadth ~60-80% email accuracy → always validate; Clay waterfall for quality; Sales Nav for DM mapping, manual only).
- **Phase 3 qualify:** every qualification gets evidence (1-2 source URLs). Confidence: High = 2 independent sources; Medium = 1 credible + consistent search evidence; Low = flag what's uncertain.
- **Phase 4 score:** Hot = ICP fit + clear buying signal + accessible DM + verified contact. Warm = fit + softer/older signal. Cold = loose fit OR no signal OR unverified. Skip = disqualifier hit. Target ~20% Hot / 30% Warm.
- **Phase 5 lead sheet:** table ≤25 rows, CSV above; always append "Top outreach targets" (3-5 hot leads, one-sentence rationale naming the trigger + DM: "Hired new VP Marketing 14 days ago; verified email; mid-market fit").
- **Top-target ranking:** trigger recency (30 days > 6 months) > trigger specificity > decision-maker access > vertical precision.
- **SaaS branch:** technographic (competing tool = switch play; complementary = integration play; DIY tooling = buyer signal) + growth signals (funding <6mo, 10%+ headcount growth, hiring your buyer's role). Decay: layoffs in target dept, no shipping 6mo, founders-only team.
- **B2B (non-SaaS) branch:** PE ownership + acquisition activity matter more than funding rounds. Use size bands, never point revenue estimates. Never cold-email executive assistants.
- **Dev-tool branch:** GitHub stargazers/forks of 3-5 anchor repos (competitors + adjacent tools) = highest-quality intent; email yield only 5-20% but signal beats firmographics.
- **Hot label without a buying signal is forbidden.** ICP fit alone is not timing.

## Personalization at scale (marketingskills/cold-email/personalization.md)
- **Four levels:** L1 merge tags (~5% lift, table stakes) → L2 industry/segment pains → L3 role-level → L4 individual observation tied to the problem (gold standard; up to +250% replies).
- **The remove-the-opener test:** if you delete the personalized opening and the email still makes sense, the personalization isn't working. It must segue into why you're reaching out.
- **The "So what?" test:** read every opener from the prospect's side. If the answer to "why would I care?" is nothing, rewrite.
- **3-minute system (30MPC):** (1) stack-rank top 10 buying signals - 5 company, 5 person; (2) 3x3 template: personalization-tied-to-problem / problem you solve / one-line solution + low-friction CTA; (3) pre-write 5 trigger-template paragraphs with segues. Personalized email in <3 minutes.
- **Signal stack:** funding (Crunchbase/press), job postings (careers page), tech stack (BuiltWith/Wappalyzer), LinkedIn activity, company news, podcast/talks, website changes - each phrased as observation → implied challenge.
- **Psychographic > firmographic** for response rates (Becc Holland's four -graphics). What they're passionate about outperforms what size they are.
- **Fake personalization is worse than none:** "Loved your post!" without specifics, "I saw your LinkedIn profile," compliment-then-pivot attention hacks.

## Message construction (marketingskills/cold-email + agency-agents)
- **Subject lines: 2-4 words, lowercase, internal camouflage.** Colleague-looking subjects double opens (Gong). 2-word = +60% opens vs 5-word. First name in subject = -12% replies. {{painPoint}}/{{competitor}} context beats {{firstName}}. Default to statements; only highly specific pain questions work.
- **Subject anti-patterns with costs:** salesy words -17.9% opens; "!!!" -36%; numbers/percentages -46%; product pitch in subject -57% replies; fake "Re:"/"Fwd:" destroys trust.
- **Body: 4 lines / under 150 words hard cap, 25-75 words preferred.** Anatomy: signal-based opener → value in THEIR vocabulary (one sentence, concrete outcome) → optional one-line proof ("[similar co] cut [metric] by [number] in [timeframe]") → single low-friction CTA.
- **Performance levers, ranked:** hook type (timeline hooks 3.4x problem hooks in meetings) > personalization depth > brevity > targeting precision > follow-up > 3rd-5th-grade reading level (+67%) > Thursday sends (6.87% peak).
- **Count your I/we sentences.** "We are a leading…" = delete signal. One proof point beats ten features.
- **Calibrate by culture:** US direct/short; UK insight-led; DACH precise/data-driven; Scandinavia fact-based. NA replies 4.1% vs EU 3.1% vs APAC 2.8% - adjust expectations per region.
- **Frameworks stay in the toolkit** (AIDA/PAS/BAB/QVC/4-line from alirezarezvani, retained; plus Mouse Trap, 3C's, SCQ, Vanilla Ice Cream per marketingskills/frameworks.md). Frameworks teach thinking; never paste as template.

## Sequence architecture (marketingskills/follow-up-sequences.md + agency-agents)
- **55% of replies come from follow-ups; 48% of senders never follow up once.** First follow-up alone adds +49% replies.
- **Cap at 4 follow-ups (5 emails total).** By the 4th follow-up, responses drop 55% and spam complaints triple. 4-7-email campaigns hit 27% reply vs 9% for 1-3 - the cap balances both.
- **Cadence with increasing gaps:** Day 0 → 3 → 7-8 → 14 → 21-28. Waiting 3 days before follow-up 1 = +31% response. Tue-Thu sends, 9-11am or 1-3pm prospect-local. Avoid Monday AM and Friday PM.
- **Angle rotation, one new value prop per email:** initial hook+value → new insight/stat → social proof/case study → industry trend/resource → breakup. Each email stands alone (they may not have read earlier ones).
- **Multi-channel mapping by persona:** C-suite LinkedIn/InMail-first + warm intro; VP email-first; Director email→phone; Manager email→LinkedIn→Loom; technical buyers technical-content email + community. 
- **Default cold sequence: 5-8 touches over 21 days** (email + LinkedIn connect-no-pitch + insight email + phone/VM + content email + breakup). **Tier-1 accounts: up to 10 touches / 28 days** adding LinkedIn engagement, 60-second personalized Loom, and a new-stakeholder-angle email (agency-agents 10-touch architecture).
- **Breakup email structure:** acknowledge attempts → validate their silence → state it's the final email → leave door open. Optional 1-2-3 reply-with-a-number format. **If you send a breakup, you never contact again** (90-day nurture re-engagement is a new, signal-justified thread).
- **Banned follow-up phrases:** "I never heard back" (-12% bookings, Gong), "just checking in," "bumping this up," "did you see my last email?"

## Reply handling (agency-agents/sales-outreach.md)
- **Positive reply** → respond <1h business hours, confirm next step, qualify briefly (abbreviated BANT/MEDDIC - frameworks live with Sales Engineer, don't duplicate), book within 24h, calendar link now.
- **Objection = engagement.** Curiosity before rebuttal - restate, ask, then answer:
  - "No budget" → "No budget existing, or none allocated yet?" + how peers found it (ROI reframe / consolidation / planning-cycle timing).
  - "Already use [competitor]" → "What made you choose them? Anything you wish worked differently?" Never badmouth.
  - "Not a priority" → "What IS the top priority this quarter?" - disqualify gracefully if no fit.
  - "Send me info" → qualify with 2 questions first, then send tailored material, never a generic deck.
  - "No time to implement" → ask their typical process, answer with timeframe + lift evidence.
  - "Too expensive" → "Outside budget entirely, or value question?" then ROI walk-through.
- **"Not interested"** → suppress, polite exit, ask if timing is the issue; 6-month re-engagement reminder only if they signaled timing.
- **"Wrong person"** → thank + ask for referral to the right person.
- **Sequence exhausted, no reply** → nurture tag, 90-day re-engagement with a NEW signal, never a re-run of the same sequence.
- **Every touch + response + next action logged.** A disorganized pipeline is a leaking pipeline. Warm replies hand off to Sales Engineer with full context (signal, thread, objections raised).

## Deliverability for cold (deepened 2026-06-09)
- **New sending domain:** SPF + DKIM + DMARC before anything; 2-4 week warm-up (Lemwarm/Instantly/Mailwarm); subdomain isolation (cold from team.domain.com).
- **Volume:** 50-100/day per inbox cap; rotate multiple inboxes; ramp gradually post-warm-up.
- **Hard thresholds:** bounce <2% (excellent) - >2% = stop and re-validate list; spam complaints - Google enforces at 0.1%, treat 0.05% as the alarm line: pause, audit, remediate.
- **Every address validated before send** (Truelist/NeverBounce/ZeroBounce class). Guessed emails sent unverified destroy sender reputation AND void a GDPR legitimate-interest claim.
- **Plain-text or HTML-light, one link max, no attachments first touch, no spam-trigger words** (free/urgent/act now - "Act Now" stacking = +67% spam flagging).
- **Blacklist checks weekly.** Reply-rate <2-3% sustained = ICP/list/message audit, not more volume.

## Compliance (marketingskills/prospecting/compliance.md + standing)
- **Every contact carries source URL + collection date + lawful basis.** Required for GDPR DSARs and CAN-SPAM defense; capture at list-build time, not retroactively.
- **GDPR legitimate interest (B2B cold):** business role + public business-context source + clear opt-out + the test written down. First touch includes privacy notice/opt-out.
- **CASL (Canada):** publicly-published business address is the defensible cold basis; sender ID + mailing address + unsubscribe in every message.
- **CAN-SPAM:** unsubscribe honored within 10 business days; physical address present.
- **Never:** bulk-scrape LinkedIn/Google Maps (Sales Nav manual research only; permanent bans), buy broker lists without provenance, harvest personal Gmail addresses, store Maps Place IDs, resell extracted data, bypass CAPTCHAs/login walls.

## Metrics & diagnostics (marketingskills/benchmarks.md + agency-agents)
- **Benchmarks:** open 40-45% good / 50%+ excellent; reply 5-10% good / 10-15% excellent (signal-based with research: 12-25%); positive-reply share 55-60% good; meeting rate 1-2% good / 2.3%+ excellent; meeting conversion 40-60% of positive replies; sequence completion 80%+.
- **Funnel reality check:** 500 emails → ~25 replies → ~8 positive → ~4 meetings → ~1 client (≈0.2% e2e for average senders). Plan capacity accordingly.
- **Diagnose by stage:** low open = subject/deliverability; opens but no replies = message relevance/length/CTA; replies but negative = ICP or offer; positive replies but no meetings = response speed or friction in the ask.
- **Reply-rate tiers as a quality audit:** 1-3% = generic (fix targeting + personalization); 5-8% = role-personalized; 12-25% = signal-based; if results don't match the effort tier you claim, the personalization is cosmetic.
- **Test one variable at a time, 200+ sends per arm.** Changing subject + opener + CTA simultaneously teaches nothing.
- **Quantify recommendations:** "DevOps sequence reply dropped 14%→6% after touch 3; the case-study email is the weak link" - never "we should optimize."

## Channel strategy guardrails (agency-agents/lead-gen-strategist, light)
- **Cold outreach is one of the Core Four** (warm outreach / content / cold / paid). Validate the offer via warm outreach before scaling it cold; dominate one channel before adding a second.
- **Consistency rule (Rule of 100):** sustained daily activity beats sporadic bursts - most outbound fails on insufficient consistent reach, not cleverness. Within deliverability caps, always.

## Red flags
- Sending from main domain; unwarmed domains; bought lists (60% bounce + reputation damage)
- Subject lines: "Quick question", "Following up", "Touching base"; fake Re:/Fwd:
- Multiple CTAs; HTML-heavy; attachments on first touch; 30-minute-call ask in touch 1
- Generic personalization (first name only); compliment-then-pivot openers; "Hope this finds you well"
- No follow-up cadence (one-and-done) or no break-up email
- Sequences continuing after "not interested"; contacting after a sent breakup
- 10+ contacts per company; Hot-scored leads without a buying signal; unverified emails in the send list
- "Just bumping this up" follow-ups - value beats nag, every touch

## Decision rules (When → Do)
- **When** new outbound campaign → narrow the ICP first. "Founders at fintech SaaS, 10-100 employees, raised Series A in last 90 days" beats "founders." Then write the falsifiable pass/fail checklist.
- **When** a T1/T2 buying signal fires → outreach within 30 minutes, referencing the signal in line 1.
- **When** building a list → 2-3x oversource, cross-verify 2+ sources, validate every email, capture source URL + date per contact.
- **When** writing the email → 4 lines max: signal hook, why-now in their vocabulary, one proof point, soft CTA. Read aloud before sending.
- **When** subject line → 2-4 words, lowercase, internal-looking. Testing: 1 variable, 200+ sends per arm.
- **When** sequencing → 5 emails max on increasing gaps (0/3/7/14/21-28) with channel mix per persona; Tier-1 accounts up to 10 touches/28 days.
- **When** positive reply → respond <1h, qualify briefly, book within 24h; calendar link in the second message, not the first.
- **When** objection → restate, ask a curious question, then answer. Scripts in Reply handling above.
- **When** "not interested" → suppress + polite exit. **When** "wrong person" → referral ask. **When** silence after full sequence → nurture tag, 90-day signal-based re-engagement.
- **When** reply rate <2-3% sustained → stop sending; audit ICP, list quality, and message tier before adding volume.
- **When** bounce >2% or complaints ≥0.05% → pause sends, re-validate list, check blacklists, remediate before resuming.
- **When** new sending domain → SPF/DKIM/DMARC + 2-4 week warm-up before a single cold send; 50-100/day/inbox cap after.
- **When** a lead goes warm and technical → hand off to Sales Engineer with signal, thread, and objection context attached.

## Standing gotchas
- Buying a list → ~60% bounce, immediate sender-reputation damage, and no GDPR lawful basis. Never.
- "Hope this finds you well" / "I came across your profile" - universal delete signals and AI-email tells; cut every time.
- Personalization that doesn't connect to the problem is an attention hack - it gets opens, not replies.
- Apollo/ZoomInfo data is stale more often than you think (~60-80% email accuracy) - re-verify before any Hot score.
- Long sequences feel productive but by follow-up 4 responses are down 55% and complaints have tripled - the cap exists for sender-reputation survival.
- Breakup emails outperform mid-sequence touches (10-15% response) precisely because they're honest - don't fake-breakup as a tactic and then keep sending.
- Multi-threading ≠ mass-emailing the org chart: 1-2 contacts/company doubles the reply rate vs 10+.

## Cold calling + 2026 deliverability + booking (v0.6.0 depth - see cold-call-deliverability-2026.md)
- On calls: lead with a permission-based ~27-sec opener; handle objections by bucket (Agree → Incentivize → Sell the test drive); talk:listen ~40:60; sell the MEETING; always leave a voicemail. A "not interested" email = call them, don't re-email.
- Deliverability is now SMTP-level rejection: never cold-send from the primary domain; warmed secondary domains, 25-40/inbox/day, SPF+DKIM+DMARC aligned, list verified, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Watch the per-provider 5,000/day permanent threshold.
- Never book a meeting that fails the 5-criteria gate (ICP, authority, quoted pain, real timing signal, committee map). Attach a pre-meeting brief on handoff; verify attendee data to kill no-shows.

## AI-native lead intelligence + warm-path / network growth (v0.8.0 depth - see lead-intelligence-and-network.md)
- For high-value targets (operators/founders/investors) or when vendor data is thin, score the PERSON 0-100 from agent web+social search (role 30 / industry 25 / activity 20 / influence 10 / location 10 / engagement 5) instead of buying a list. The score feeds Hot/Warm/Cold/Skip but never overrides the buying-signal-required-for-Hot rule. Do not fabricate profile data; flag sparse-data scores.
- Before cold-emailing a high-value target, map mutuals (bridge ranking) and find the shortest warm chain (direct mutual > portfolio/advisory > co-worker/alumni > event overlap > content engagement). If a Tier 1/2 bridge exists, draft a warm-intro request to the mutual FIRST. Only report connections you can verify.
- Channel-by-warmth (refines persona map): warm-intro email > direct email > LinkedIn DM > X DM; one primary channel. Word caps: 60 intro / 80 cold email / 40 DM. Run brand-voice first; anti-slop bans (no "game-changer", "deep dive", "leverage", "synergy", etc.); drafts only, never auto-send.

Review-first, never blind auto-prune; X prune only accounts you follow (never followers); LinkedIn 1st-degree removal is manual-review-first. Modes: light-pass / default / aggressive. Triggers: "clean up my network", "who should I unfollow/follow/reconnect with".
