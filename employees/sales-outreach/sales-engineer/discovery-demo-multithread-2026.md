# Discovery + demo + multi-threading - depth reference

METHODOLOGY absorption (2026-06-13, web research synthesis; sources: Gong Labs 67,149-demo study + 7.1M-opportunity AI analysis, Winning by Design SPICED, MEDDIC Academy, RAIN Group, Apollo technical-sales, Gartner). Gate-0: v0.5.0 owned the qualification frameworks (MEDDIC/BANT) by design but lacked the quantified demo mechanics, SPICED discovery prompts, the multi-threading data, and the technical-buyer playbook. This adds the running-the-motion depth.

## 1. Discovery - run the frameworks, don't just name them
- **SPICED** (Winning by Design; mid-market sequence): Situation → Pain → Impact → Critical Event → Decision. Working prompts:
  - Situation: "I saw you opened an Austin office - how's that changing your hiring timeline?"
  - Pain: "Walk me through what happens today when a rep needs [X]. Where does it break?"
  - Impact: "If that's 3 hrs instead of 30 min, what does that cost across 20 reps a week?"
  - Critical Event: "Is there a board review or renewal date driving the timeline?"
- **MEDDIC depth** (enterprise): the practice that matters is confirming a REAL Economic Buyer + Champion and multi-threading - not filling an acronym. Add C (Competition→MEDDICC) for RFPs, Paper Process (MEDDPICC) for procurement-heavy. Rigorous MEDDIC drops forecast variance from 30-50% to <10%.

## 2. The demo - Gong's 67,149-demo study (the most actionable demo data)
- **Winning demos run LONGER: ~47 min vs ~36 for losers** - because of more DIALOGUE, not more monologue.
- **76-second rule:** no closed-won demo had >76s of uninterrupted pitching (losers ran to 106s). Break it up.
- **Speaker switches:** winners had 21% more switches/min. Talk:listen ~65:35 but in SHORT bursts.
- **Solution mapping = #1 lever:** show features in the PRIORITY ORDER of the pains from discovery. Showing your favorite feature first ("upside-down demo") is a top reason deals stall.
- **47-min agenda:** Welcome 0-3 → Discovery recap (restate pains in priority order, reconfirm) 3-10 → Tailored features (3, mapped to pains) 10-25 → Interactive (prospect drives "what if") 25-35 → Next steps (BOOK the follow-up before hanging up) 35-40.
- **Per feature: Show-Tell-Ask** - show it in action → tell them what it means for THEIR problem in THEIR words → ask if it lands. (Manufactures the speaker switches.)
- Say "let me show you how [Company] would see [their metric] on day one," not "let me show you our dashboard." Pre-load every tab, industry-matched dummy data, never build a report/run a query live.
- **5 deal-killers:** feature dumping, monologuing past 76s, skipping the discovery recap, demoing to one person, ending with "I'll send materials."

## 3. Multi-threading (highest-leverage win-rate move)
- 77% of deals involve multiple contacts; closed-won deals have ~2x the buyer contacts of lost; won-deal selling teams 67% larger; enterprise deals avg 17 contacts. **Multi-threading lifts win rates 130% on deals over $50K.**
- Ask the champion: "Who else needs to see this before a decision? Can we get [technical lead] and [economic buyer] on separate sessions?" Run SEPARATE tailored sessions: technical deep-dive for IT, ROI for finance, workflow for end users.
- **Bring an SE in for technical validation on enterprise deals - up to +30% win rate.** (~3 demos to close mid-market; up to 15 across stakeholders for enterprise.)

## 4. Selling to technical buyers (developers/IT/security)
- They smell a pitch instantly and distrust marketing speak. Win with specificity over hype, accurate stack/terminology, and proof you did the homework (architecture, GitHub, tech blog). Verify TECHNOLOGY FIT before booking (BuiltWith/Wappalyzer or direct confirmation) - incompatible stacks are slow/dead deals.
- Document constraints: integration requirements, security/compliance mandates, performance SLAs (MEDDIC / BANT+Technical Fit). Don't trash the incumbent - trap questions so they surface the gap themselves.
- Macro: Gartner - 61% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience; interactive self-serve demos convert ~7.9x higher on-site. Use ungated interactive demos to qualify BEFORE a human call (replaces the FIRST live demo, not the closing one) and to feed async stakeholders.

## 5. Proposal-to-close + follow-up discipline
- Healthy proposal close rate 30-50%; below that the close process is broken. COMPRESS proposal-to-signature time (10 days → 3 closes higher - momentum). Standardize proposal sections + pricing guardrails.
- 80% of sales need 5+ touches; most close between follow-up 3 and 5. Same-day recap email (their pains in their words + next step → gives the champion something to forward), then a VALUE touch day 3 (relevant article/intro - give before you ask). Never end any stage with "I'll send materials" - book the next step live.
- Conversion benchmarks: Demo→Qualified 60-70% (elite 90%+); Qualified→Meeting 50-60% (70%+); demo talk:listen 65:35 (elite 55:45); SQL→Closed B2B SaaS ~37% (elite 50%+).
- AI usage is a performance variable: sellers who frequently use AI generate 77% more revenue (Gong 7.1M-opportunity). AI's job = prep, recaps, CRM - not running the demo.
