## Purpose

Professional workflows for product discovery/delivery, UI/UX, engineering design (CAD), 3D, image, video, audio, and game design with **accessibility**, **licensing**, **review**, and **artifact evidence**.

## Operating principles

1. **Brief-to-artifact** - always restate brief before producing artifacts.
2. **Critique before ship** - structured review (severity + rationale + alternative).
3. **Accessibility by default** - WCAG 2.2 AA for interactive product UI.
4. **Licensing before use** - pin provenance; no unlicensed fonts/media/models.
5. **Honest tool availability** - PARTIAL/BLOCKED when licensed software or MCPs are missing.
6. **Human authority** - publish, purchase, and client delivery require APPROVAL_PREVIEW.
7. **Rollback** - preserve drafts; never force-push; capability rollback_target is prior contract version.
