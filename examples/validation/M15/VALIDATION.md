## M15 oracle (oracle/m15_oracle.py, black box)

Units and events are generated by the oracle (arms base and variant, May 2026 cohort, 10-day
horizon). Counts, the Wald interval, the pooled z test and the SRM chi-square are computed here with
statistics.NormalDist and math.erfc. Core rows:

- X01 A/A (90/300 each): no_detectable_difference, no claim. X02 15% vs 24%: exact counts, interval
  and p value equal my computation to 1e-6, causal claim.
- Repeated users: X03 nine sessions per converting unit and 40 repeat assignment rows count each
  unit once. X17 a unit first assigned before the cohort and again inside it stays outside.
- Leakage: X04 30 purchases 1 s before assignment and 10 just after the horizon do not count, a
  purchase at the assignment instant does, 7 unassigned units' events are ignored and counted.
  X05 20 units in both arms (exactly 5%) are excluded and named without invalidating; 21 invalidate.
- Underpowered: X06 149 vs 400 units with 5% vs 50% is insufficient_information with no claim;
  150 per arm is read.
- X07 SRM 330/450 invalid; 380/420 not; 240/560 against a planned 30/70 not; chi-square equal to mine.
- X08 observational: association_only, significance only. X09 13 outside-cohort units and 5 immature
  units excluded and counted, not failures. X15 missing values and X16 an unknown arm excluded and
  counted. X10 nine invalid packages (including a duplicate key) exit 2, previous `--out` kept, then
  recovery. X11 shuffled rows give the same report. X12 folder unchanged, no bytecode from the G13 import.

Oracle history, disclosed: v1 (oracle/m15_oracle_v1.py) assumed an event exactly at assignment +
horizon counts; the doc does not say, so v2 accepts either reading and records it (X04b). v2 teeth

## Findings

- **P2-1 A ramped allocation produces a causal claim with the wrong sign (Simpson).** X13b (OBS, the
  M15 goal's Simpson and cohort-mix example). In week 1 base gets 400 units and variant 100, both at a
  high base rate (0.50 vs 0.55). From week 2 base gets 100 and variant 400, at a low base rate (0.10 vs
  0.15). The variant is 5 points better in both periods. The pooled split is exactly 500/500, so SRM
  passes. The tool reports difference_detected, difference -0.190000, interval [-0.246852,
  -0.133148], `claims_allowed {causal: true, significance: true}`. X13 is the mirror case: the true
  +0.05 is reported as a causal +0.29.
  The G15 acceptance says "no causal or significance claim unsupported by supplied experiment", and
  `claims_allowed` is what a PM or exec summary may repeat. The data needed to catch this (assigned_at)
  is in the package. I graded it P2, not P3, because the claim is emitted as allowed, not just
  labelled. The limits do mention "covariate adjustment", but that is about variance, not this.
- **P4-1 Horizon end is exclusive, not documented.** X04b: an event exactly at assignment +
  horizon_days does not count. The doc says "within the horizon"; the builder's test pins exclusive.
  Suggested: write `[assigned_at, assigned_at + horizon)` in the doc.

## Proposed fix for P2-1 (proposed-fix-allocation-drift.patch, not applied)

The patch is +26/-1 in experiment_readout.py and experiment-readout.md. It runs the existing SRM test
per assignment week. Only weeks with at least 20 mature cohort units are tested, against p < 0.001
divided by the number of weeks tested. A failing week gives invalid_experiment with a trace naming
the week, and `allocation_by_week` is added to the JSON.

Positive control (scripts/fix_control.sh, results/fix-positive-control.txt), applied with `git apply`
to a fresh copy, on 3.9.6 and 3.13.15:

- X13 and X13b become invalid_experiment.
- The three README fixtures keep their outcomes.
- G15 tests OK, full suite OK, builder oracle 12/12, M15 oracle 15/15 core.
- Builder oracle seeds 1 to 8 (quick) are 11/11 each, so there are no false invalidations on its
  generated experiments (results/fix-seeds-quick.txt).

A first control run did not apply the patch because of a path-prefix error in the patch file. It
is kept as results/kept/fix-positive-control-patch-not-applied.txt and is not evidence. If the builder
adopts this patch, any later scoring of the fix must say it came from this Grok patch.
