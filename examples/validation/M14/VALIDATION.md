## M14 oracle (oracle/m14_oracle.py, black box)

Books are generated by the oracle (July 2026 period, as_of 2026-08-06, CHF, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY) and
valued by an independent as-of journal written here with Decimal. Expectations come from
cash-revenue-reconciliation.md only. Core rows:

- K01 clean control exit 0, exact. K02 three seeded 60-invoice books in USD, EUR and zero-decimal JPY
  (partial payments, credit notes, refunds, delivery before and after issue, undelivered invoices)
  equal the journal on every figure and both balance dates.
- Settlement timing: K03 a payment 1 s before the period settles opening AR, one at the start is
  period cash, one at the period end (before as_of) is not. K18 an invoice issued exactly at the period
  end is not billed in the period.
- Negative and duplicate transactions: K04 a negative payment and a zero credit note are rejected with
  line and reason; an exact repeat (later loaded_at) counts once; one id with two amounts excludes both
  and is named; two different ids with the same content both count.
- Mixed currencies: K05 identical amounts in USD, EUR and GBP stay apart, lower-case `usd` joins USD, a
  USD payment against an EUR invoice is unapplied USD (`currency_mismatch`). K19 a ledger currency
  missing from the statement and a cash-out difference each give exit 1.
- Missing documents: K06 no reference, unknown invoice, credit note on an unknown invoice and one
  dated before its invoice, an invoice without loaded_at and one without delivery are all listed and
  kept out of revenue. K15 a void invoice is not billed and its payment is unapplied. K16 an empty
  amount is a rejected row, not zero. K17 an invoice excluded as conflicting cannot take a payment.
- Late and refund: K07 a payment loaded after as_of is late and excluded, and the bank control shows
  the 15.00 difference; a refund moves cash and AR only; K07b a later cutoff takes the row in.
- K08 credit note after delivery is contra-revenue, before delivery reduces deferred; overpayment is a
  customer credit; delivery before issue is unbilled. All three checks close.
- K09 every row re-delivered and shuffled gives the same figures; rerun byte-identical. K10 six invalid
  packages exit 2 and keep the previous `--out`, then recovery. K11 a three-decimal amount and a
  non-number are rejected; JPY works. K12 a bank-only currency and a 0.01 difference give exit 1.
  K13 package folder unchanged and no bytecode left from the G13 import.

Oracle history, disclosed: v1 (oracle/m14_oracle_v1.py) had two harness errors, not tool faults: K06
required the literal reason `no_reference` (the doc says "no reference"; the tool says

## Findings

- **P3-1 Over-refund and over-credit pass as a clean reconciliation.** K14 (OBS): an invoice of 100.00,
  paid 100.00, then a 500.00 refund and a 300.00 credit note gives exit 0, no exceptions, recognized
  revenue -200.00 and AR 200.00 owed. The arithmetic follows the documented rules and nothing is
  dropped, but a refund five times what was paid and negative revenue on one invoice are what the M14
  goal means by challenging negative transactions, and the report gives the CFO no flag. The handback
  raises the credit-note half as an open question. Suggested: list refunds above the amount applied
  and credit notes above the invoice as exceptions (exit 1).
- **P4-1 Unapplied reasons in the doc are not the emitted names.** The doc says "no reference, unknown,
  void, rejected or late invoice, currency mismatch"; the tool emits `no_invoice_reference`,
  `unknown_invoice`, `invoice_void`, `invoice_conflicting_versions` and `currency_mismatch`. A consumer
  writing against the doc will guess wrong. Suggested: list the exact values.
