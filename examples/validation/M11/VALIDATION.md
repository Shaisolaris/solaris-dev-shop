## Findings

- **P3-1 Conflict rule can hide the only error minutes.** probes/p_per_source.py. A bundle where
  two load balancers each report their share of every minute is read as duplicates when the shares
  are equal (counted once, so the denominator is halved with no label) and as a conflict when they
  differ. In the probe the one minute with errors differed, so it was left out and the SLO read
  `met`, availability 1.000000, coverage 0.983333, with one "conflicting telemetry" label. The
  behaviour follows the documented rules and is labelled, and the schema says one count per minute,
  so it is P3 and not P2. The doc does not say what `source` means or that per-source rows must be
  summed first. Suggested: document that, or refuse a minute whose records come from different
  sources with different counts instead of dropping it while the status can still read `met`.
- **P4-1 Equal per-source counts are merged silently.** Same probe, second case: two sources with
  identical counts are one record, no label. Same remedy as P3-1.
- **P4-2 Burn-since-change window starts at the change's minute.** E18 (OBS): the trace shows the
  burn measured from 13:30:00 for a change at 13:30:40, so the 40 s before the change count. The doc
  says "from the change". It does not change any decision in the fixtures or the oracle.
