# Fares
Fares were last reviewed on 1 October 2026.
A single adult ride costs 2.75 dollars on any bus or ferry.
Riders aged 65 or older pay a reduced fare of 1.35 dollars per ride.
Children under 6 ride free when travelling with a paying adult.
A 30-day pass costs 98 dollars and covers unlimited bus and ferry rides.
Transfers between buses are free for 90 minutes after the first tap.
